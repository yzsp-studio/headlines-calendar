## 🇨🇳 中文

这是一个面向新媒体运营的标题与选题策划技能：把文章、素材、账号定位甚至一句模糊方向，转成可发布的跨平台标题与选题日历。它按账号定位、受众、产能和营销节点，规划周度或月度内容排期，并对标题做生成、改写、评估与 A/B 对比。产出会真实创建、写入并校验飞书/Lark 文档后返回可访问链接，不会静默降级成 Markdown、Word、PDF 或聊天文本。单个任务总标题数控制在 30 个以内。它只做标题与日历，不代写完整文章、小说、脚本或 PPT；能力范围外或多意图任务会先说明边界，再给出可转化、分模块的交付方案。

### ✨ 能做什么

- 输入文章/素材/账号定位 → 输出跨平台可发布标题组
- 同批标题做拆解、评估与 A/B 对比，按平台规则适配改写
- 按账号定位、受众、产能与营销节点排周度或月度选题日历
- 规划栏目与热点日历，把节点提前写进排期
- 单次总标题数不超过 30 个，超范围先划边界再分模块交付
- 真实创建、写入并校验飞书/Lark 文档，返回可访问链接

## 🇬🇧 English

A headline and editorial-calendar skill for social media teams: it turns articles, raw material, account positioning or a vague direction into publishable cross-platform titles and a topic calendar. It plans weekly or monthly schedules around account positioning, audience, production capacity and campaign dates, and generates, rewrites, scores and A/B-tests titles. The result is actually created, written and validated as a Feishu/Lark document with a working link returned, never silently downgraded to Markdown, Word, PDF or chat text. A single run caps at 30 titles. It deliberately stops short of writing full articles, novels, scripts or slide decks, and out-of-scope or multi-intent requests are met with a clear boundary plus a modular delivery plan.

### ✨ What it does

- Article, raw material or account positioning in → publishable cross-platform titles out
- Break down, score and A/B-test a title set, with per-platform rewrites
- Weekly or monthly topic calendar built around positioning, audience, capacity and campaign dates
- Column planning and a hot-topic calendar that puts key dates on the schedule early
- Hard cap of 30 titles per run; out-of-scope work gets a boundary note and modular plan
- Creates, writes and validates a real Feishu/Lark doc, then returns a working link
